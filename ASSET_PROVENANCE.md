# Artwork provenance

`static/world-island.png` was generated with the OpenAI image-generation tool for this KULT World frontend. It is decorative World-map artwork, not a screenshot or a data visualization. No external brand logo or partnership badge is included.

Creative direction: a polished isometric floating island town at night, navy surroundings with mint/cyan and lavender light, a cozy home in the upper left, observatory at the top, lab in the upper right, workshop in the lower left, arena in the lower right and a central plaza. No lettering; interactive district names and controls are rendered in HTML.

The image was visually inspected and included locally so the app does not rely on a third-party image host. Asset path: `static/world-island.png`.
